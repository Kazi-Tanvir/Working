# Risk Register

> Risk scoring: **L** = Low, **M** = Medium, **H** = High. Every High-impact risk has both a **Mitigation** (prevent it) and a **Fallback** (what to do on showcase day if it happens anyway).

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Fallback |
|----|------|-----------|--------|------------|----------|
| R01 | **Venue Wi-Fi is missing, blocked, or captive-portal gated** | H | H | Never rely on venue Wi-Fi. Pre-configure `config.h` with a team member's phone hotspot SSID/password and test it at home. | Run ESP32 in **offline mode** (sensors + OLED + buttons + auto-threshold still work) and show pre-recorded demo video for cloud/voice. |
| R02 | **Venue noise breaks voice recognition** | H | H | Use push-to-talk (click mic, speak 30 cm from mic, click stop). Test under crowd noise before showcase. Bring a wired/USB headset mic if available. | Switch immediately to the **Typed Command** input on the dashboard, or physical buttons + pre-recorded voice video. |
| R03 | **MQ-135 not warmed up in time for judging** | M | M | Burn-in MQ-135 for 24–48 h at home before Dec 11. On showcase morning, power on the desk circuit **at least 30–45 min** before judges arrive. | Explain the heater warm-up requirement honestly; show raw ADC trend when breathing near sensor without claiming calibrated ppm. |
| R04 | **MQ-135 AOUT exceeds 3.3 V and damages ESP32 GPIO34** | L | H | Always build the 1 kΩ + 1.5 kΩ voltage divider first and verify ≤ 3.0 V with a multimeter *before* connecting to GPIO34. | Swap to the **spare ESP32** (pre-flashed) and re-check divider resistor values before powering on. |
| R05 | **ESP32 brown-out / random resets from underpowered USB** | M | H | Power the 5V rail (MQ-135 heater + relay coil + ESP32 VIN) from a **5V 2A USB charger**, not a weak laptop USB port. Share common GND. | Swap USB cable/charger; temporarily disconnect the 5V fan/load from the same supply and power load separately. |
| R06 | **Solder joint breaks in transit or at venue** | M | H | Perform full continuity check on perfboard at home; secure wires with hot glue/zip ties; carry circuit in a padded box. | Keep the **breadboard + spare ESP32** in the toolkit as an emergency drop-in backup. |
| R07 | **Vapi free tier quota exhausted or API outage** | M | M | Monitor Vapi credit balance before Dec 11; limit casual testing once M6 passes Gate 3; keep prompts concise. | Use **Typed Command** fallback on dashboard + play pre-recorded voice demo video. |
| R08 | **Railway backend or MySQL sleeps / goes down** | L | H | Check Railway service health 40 min before showcase; keep `/api/health` warm during setup; test database retention. | Run Express backend locally on Laptop 1 + SQLite, point dashboard/ESP32 (or ngrok for Vapi) to local laptop over hotspot. |
| R09 | **DHT22 returns `NaN` intermittently or fails** | M | M | Use a DHT22 breakout board (with pull-up) or add 10 kΩ pull-up resistor; firmware falls back to last-known-good value and shows `?` instead of crashing. | Re-seat jumper wires; if module is dead, demonstrate MQ-135 + relay + dashboard while noting sensor fault handling. |
| R10 | **OLED stays blank or shows garbage** | M | M | Run I2C scanner on Day 2 to confirm `0x3C` vs `0x3D`; solder headers cleanly; verify 3.3V and GND. | Show live readings on laptop **Serial Monitor** or **Next.js Dashboard** while fixing I2C wiring. |
| R11 | **Relay doesn't switch load (or switches backwards)** | M | M | Verify active-LOW vs active-HIGH logic on your specific relay module on Day 2; power relay VCC from **5V** (not 3.3V); keep load low-voltage (5V/12V DC only). | Watch the relay board's indicator LED to prove GPIO switching, or flip `LOW`/`HIGH` in `setRelay()` and reflash. |
| R12 | **Browser denies microphone permission** | M | M | Always open the dashboard over **HTTPS** (Vercel provides HTTPS automatically; `localhost` also works). Test in Chrome before judges arrive. | Click lock icon in Chrome address bar → Allow Mic; if still blocked, use **Typed Command** fallback. |
| R13 | **ADC2 conflict kills MQ-135 readings when Wi-Fi is on** | L | H | Strictly locked MQ-135 to **GPIO34 (ADC1_CH6)** in pin map. Never move an analog read to ADC2 (GPIOs 0, 2, 4, 12–15, 25–27). | Confirm `PIN_MQ135 == 34` in `config.h` and `hardware/pin-map.md`. |
| R14 | **Git merge conflict or accidentally committed secrets** | M | M | Use feature branches (`feature/M2-...`), never push straight to `main`, keep `.env` in `.gitignore`, review PRs before merging to `dev`. | Revert to last tagged working commit on `dev`; rotate exposed API/Vapi/Telegram keys immediately. |
| R15 | **One team member becomes sick or unavailable on showcase day** | L | H | Every module has a designated **Backup Owner**; all 4 members rehearse the 3-min demo script and know how to power on/reset the system. | Remaining 3 members cover the missing role (Presenter + Voice/Dashboard + Hardware) using printed script. |
| R16 | **Team falls behind schedule (classes/exams)** | M | M | Strict Gate checkpoints every weekend; all SHOULD-tier features (M7) act as schedule buffer. | At Gate 2/3, cut M7 (Telegram, Pomodoro, To-Do) and ship ONLY the 8 MUST criteria cleanly. |
| R17 | **Heap fragmentation / ESP32 watchdog crash after long runtime** | L | M | Avoid dynamic `String` churn in tight loops; set 3s HTTP timeouts; run 2-hour soak test in Week 3. | Power-cycle ESP32 cleanly between judge waves (reboots in < 5 s and auto-reconnects). |
